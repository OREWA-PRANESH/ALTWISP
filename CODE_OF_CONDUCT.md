# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss ideas and implementations
without personal attacks. Respect privacy and differing experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publishing
another person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation proportionately.
Report misconduct privately to a maintainer through an available private contact
channel; do not reveal affected people's information in public reports. This policy
does not promise a response time or a private channel that has not been established.
