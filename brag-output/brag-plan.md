# Brag Plan: ALTWISP

## What is this app?
ALTWISP is Windows voice dictation that captures speech from a global shortcut and inserts the finished words into the application already in use, with either Groq cloud transcription or local Whisper.

## Planning rubric
1. **App:** Windows voice dictation that stays out of the way and returns words to the active text field.
2. **Strongest claim:** “Your fastest interface is already with you.”
3. **Visual hook:** The violet-and-cyan fluid orb floating over a dark signal field.
4. **Actual UI to show:** The Ctrl + Windows quick-capture control, listening status, and “Your latest words” dashboard result.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20 seconds; enough to show entry → capture → result without turning the video into a feature list.
6. **Tone:** polished with cinematic scale; premium restraint, bold type, and confident holds.
7. **Audio:** steady clean music bed with three restrained motion-matched accents and a subtle orb glow response.
8. **Share caption:** “ALTWISP turns a thought into text without making you leave the app you are already using. Voice, uninterrupted.”
9. **User flow:** Focus a text field → press Ctrl + Windows and speak → press again and see polished text land in context.

## The angle
Typing interrupts a thought; ALTWISP preserves it. The video follows one sentence across the entire product flow: it begins as an unseen thought, activates the orb through the shortcut, becomes a live listening signal, and resolves as polished text inside the real dashboard language. The product is presented as a quiet instrument rather than an AI spectacle.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
On near-black, the words “Speak.” and “Move.” arrive with generous space. The ALTWISP orb wakes between them, then the complete line resolves: “Keep going.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- Ctrl and Windows keys depress together; the floating orb lights up and the status changes from Ready to Listening.
- A short spoken-thought signal travels from the orb into a faithful recreation of the desktop dashboard.
- The latest-words field types in: “Make space for the next thought.” The status resolves to Text inserted.

## Outro / punchline
ALTWISP. “Voice, uninterrupted.” A small “Windows voice dictation” label and a restrained cyan-violet glow hold on the final frame.

## User flow worth showing
Focus a text field → press and release Ctrl + Windows → speak while the orb listens → repeat the shortcut → polished text lands in the active context.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium cinematic product film for a quiet Windows utility
- Interpretation: Slow, decisive reveals; large readable typography; faithful product UI; minimal copy; no exaggerated claims or noisy effects.

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Share copy (draft)
ALTWISP turns a thought into text without making you leave the app you are already using. Voice, uninterrupted.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with polished restraint
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: 0.26 baseline volume, immediate clean opening, subtle lift into the dashboard reveal, fade under the final logo.
- Music cue guidance: bundled 109.96 BPM preset; target the dashboard reveal near 8.74s, transcript resolution near 13.11s, and final lockup near 17.47s. Use the 9.29–10.93s beat window for the short sequential UI flow.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS/bass may breathe the orb glow and background depth by a few percent. No waveform or equalizer visualization.
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft impact for the orb wake, one low-risk click for Ctrl + Win, and one warm success accent when text resolves.
- Audio-coupled moments: shortcut press, Listening status reveal, transcript completion, final logo.
- Restraint rule: sound must never overpower copy or make the utility feel like a game.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The thought — 3 seconds
Near-black field with a faint grid. “Speak.” arrives, then the orb wakes, then “Move. Keep going.” resolves around it. Use the actual ALTWISP orb asset and the website’s dark spectrum palette.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two short text beats followed by the orb wake.
Audio intent: establish quiet confidence with one soft low impact.
Audio-coupled idea: major orb wake may lock near a strong musical cue only if readability remains intact.
Music: clean, steady bed.
Transition mood: soft dramatic wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — One gesture — 5.75 seconds
A faithful dark/light ALTWISP desktop fragment enters. Ctrl and Win keys depress, the status changes from Ready to Listening, and the orb breathes with a restrained cyan-violet halo. Copy: “One gesture. Zero friction.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — Ctrl + Win press, status change, then orb response.
Audio intent: precise and tactile.
Audio-coupled idea: click at the key press; status and signal accents align to the 9.29–10.93s beat grid while readable copy remains held.
Music: steady bed with a slight lift.
Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Words land in context — 6 seconds
The dashboard becomes the hero. The “Your latest words” field types “Make space for the next thought.” The status changes from Transcribing to Text inserted. A small line below reads “Cloud or local. Your choice.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — typed result followed by completion state.
Audio intent: satisfying resolution without fanfare.
Audio-coupled idea: restrained key ticks for only the first few characters, then a warm success cue near 13.11s.
Music: continues, slightly fuller under the finished text.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Brand lockup — 5.25 seconds
The dashboard recedes into darkness while the orb remains. ALTWISP appears at full scale with “Voice, uninterrupted.” and a small “Windows voice dictation” label. Hold long enough to work as the poster frame.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: confident landing and gentle release.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo lands near 17.47s; music fades through 20s.
Music: fade to silence.
Transition mood: final hold.
